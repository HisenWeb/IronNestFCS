## IronNestFCS Smart v1.2.6

### HUD impact range restoration / HUD 打击距离恢复

- Active-gun firing-solution rows once again show the target range alongside the impact grid, azimuth, charge count, elevation and estimated flight time.
- Pending queue rows now also show the target range next to the impact grid and azimuth.
- This restores information that was accidentally dropped during the compact HUD simplification; the existing `ArtilleryTask.distance` value is used directly, with no new ballistic calculation or scheduling behavior.

- 当前炮位的射击诸元行重新显示目标距离，并继续保留打击网格、方位、装药量、仰角和预计飞行时间。
- Pending 等待队列也会在打击网格和方位旁显示目标距离。
- 本次修复恢复了紧凑 HUD 改造时意外遗漏的信息；直接使用现有的 `ArtilleryTask.distance`，不会引入新的弹道计算，也不改变任务调度行为。

### Notes / 说明

- This is a display-only patch. FirePlan execution, physical-shot settlement, two-gun matching, Auto Fire, Max Charge and F9 semantics are unchanged.
- 本版本仅修复显示。FirePlan 执行、实际击发结算、双炮匹配、Auto Fire、Max Charge 与 F9 语义均保持不变。
