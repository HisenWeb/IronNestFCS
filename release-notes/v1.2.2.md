## IronNestFCS Smart v1.2.2

### Manual fire wait / 手动击发等待

- Manual-fire missions no longer fail after a fixed 5-minute wait once the gun is prepared and waiting for the player to fire.
- With **Auto Fire** disabled, a prepared FirePlan can now remain in `WaitingForFire` indefinitely until the player fires, replans with **F9**, or the plan is otherwise cancelled.
- **Auto Fire** keeps its existing 25-second physical-fire observation timeout so failed automatic trigger attempts can still recover safely.
- Loading, elevation, turret rotation, Review / Arm and other mechanical-action timeouts are unchanged.

- 关闭 **Auto Fire** 时，火炮完成准备并等待玩家手动击发后，不再因为固定的 5 分钟等待上限而判定任务失败。
- 已准备完成的 FirePlan 现在可以一直保持 `WaitingForFire`，直到玩家实际开火、按 **F9** 重新规划，或任务因其他原因被取消。
- **Auto Fire** 仍保留原有的 25 秒实际击发观察超时，用于自动击发失败时的故障恢复。
- 装填、仰角、炮塔旋转、Review / Arm 等其他机械动作的超时逻辑保持不变。

### Notes / 说明

- This change is intended for deliberate wait-and-fire / ambush-style missions: Smart prepares the gun, while the player keeps control of when to fire.
- No Pending, dual-gun matching, loading-runtime, ballistic-planning, or F9 lifecycle behavior was changed in this release.

- 这项调整用于支持提前准备、长时间待命后再由玩家决定击发时机的任务方式：Smart 负责把火炮准备好，什么时候开火仍由玩家决定。
- 本版本没有修改 Pending、双炮匹配、装填运行时、弹道规划或 F9 生命周期行为。
